# HMO Hunter — Demo Video Script

**Format:** Screen recording walkthrough with voiceover
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Casual, showing someone how it works — like a Loom walkthrough

---

## SCENE 1 — Sign Up & Pick Your Role (0:00–0:15)

**[Screen: HMO Hunter login page. Cursor clicks "Sign Up", enters email, lands on the role selection modal with 4 cards — Investor, Council/TA, Operator, Agent.]**

> **VO:** "So first you sign up and pick your role. I'm going with Investor — but if you're a council officer, operator, or agent, the platform tailors everything to how you work."

**[Cursor clicks "Investor". Quick onboarding walkthrough flashes through, then lands on the map.]**

> **VO:** "Quick walkthrough shows you the layout, then you're straight in."

---

## SCENE 2 — The Map (0:15–0:35)

**[Screen: Full map view loads. Clusters of coloured pins across a city. Zoom smoothly into Manchester.]**

> **VO:** "This is the main map. Every pin is a property — teal ones are licensed HMOs, green are potential conversions, blue are purchase listings."

**[Cursor toggles the Article 4 overlay on. Red boundary zones appear on the map.]**

> **VO:** "I can toggle Article 4 zones on the map — these red areas show you exactly where restrictions apply, so you're not wasting time on properties you can't convert."

**[Click between category tabs: "Licensed HMOs" → "Opportunities" → "Expired Licences"]**

> **VO:** "These tabs let me jump between categories — licensed HMOs, opportunities, or expired licences that are about to come back to market."

---

## SCENE 3 — Filters (0:35–0:45)

**[Screen: Left sidebar filters open. Cursor adjusts price slider, ticks "HMO" property type, sets 5+ bedrooms, toggles EPC A-C, enables broadband filter.]**

> **VO:** "Over here I've got filters — price range, bedrooms, EPC rating, broadband speed, even floor area. The map updates as I go."

**[Map pins update in real time as filters change.]**

---

## SCENE 4 — Property Details (0:45–1:05)

**[Screen: Click a teal pin. Right sidebar slides open showing property image (Street View), deal score badge, key stats.]**

> **VO:** "Let me click into this one. Straight away I get the Street View image, a deal score out of 100, and all the key details."

**[Scroll down through the sidebar. Highlight each section briefly.]**

> **VO:** "Licence status with the expiry date, EPC rating, compliance info — it's all here without leaving the page."

**[Scroll to the Title Owner card (blue) and Licence Holder card (teal).]**

> **VO:** "This is the bit I really like — you get the title owner from Land Registry and the licence holder from the council separately. If it's a company, there's a direct link to Companies House with director details."

**[Show company name with Companies House link.]**

---

## SCENE 5 — Yield Calculator (1:05–1:20)

**[Screen: Open the Premium Yield Calculator. Room-by-room rent inputs visible.]**

> **VO:** "Now the yield calculator — I can set rents room by room using city-specific data, then plug in my costs: mortgage rate, management fees, voids."

**[Cursor adjusts a room rent. Numbers update live: gross yield, net yield, cash-on-cash return.]**

> **VO:** "Gross yield, net yield, cash-on-cash — all updating live. And I can flip to scenarios to see 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year projections."

**[Click to Scenarios tab. Show the projection columns.]**

---

## SCENE 6 — Compare & Save (1:20–1:30)

**[Screen: Quick montage — click "Compare" on 3 properties. Comparison modal opens showing side-by-side metrics: price, yield, bedrooms, EPC, Article 4 status, licence status. Best values highlighted in green.]**

> **VO:** "If I'm deciding between a few, I can compare up to three side by side — price, yield, EPC, licence status, everything lined up."

**[Show saved properties page briefly, then a saved search being loaded.]**

> **VO:** "I can also save properties and save my search filters so I can pick up where I left off."

---

## SCENE 7 — Wrap Up (1:30–1:40)

**[Screen: Zoom out to the full map showing all the pins. Pause for a beat.]**

> **VO:** "That's HMO Hunter — everything you need for HMO sourcing in one place. We're in beta right now, link's in the description."

**[Logo. URL. End.]**

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Screen Recording Checklist
- [ ] Use a clean browser (no bookmarks bar, no extensions visible)
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 100%, resolution 1920x1080
- [ ] Pre-load the map on Manchester or London with good data density
- [ ] Have 3 properties pre-selected for the comparison scene
- [ ] Ensure premium features are unlocked for the recording account
- [ ] Pre-fill the yield calculator with realistic numbers
- [ ] Disable notifications and other app popups

### Pacing Guide
| Scene | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| 1. Sign Up & Role | 15s | Show it's personalised from the start |
| 2. Map | 20s | Visual impact — pins, Article 4, tabs |
| 3. Filters | 10s | Quick power demo |
| 4. Property Details | 20s | The depth of data per property |
| 5. Yield Calc | 15s | Live numbers, scenario planning |
| 6. Compare & Save | 10s | Practical workflow |
| 7. Wrap Up | 10s | Clean exit with CTA |

### Voiceover Direction
- Pace: Relaxed, like you're showing a mate on a screen share
- Think: Loom video, not a TV ad
- Don't oversell — let the features speak for themselves
- First person throughout: "I can", "Let me", "I've got"
